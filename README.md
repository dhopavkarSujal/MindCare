# 1. Project Overview

## 1.1 Project Name

**MindCare — A Digital Mental Health & Psychological Support System for Students**

---

## 1.2 Introduction

MindCare is a digital mental-health support platform designed for college students.

The system combines **AI-guided first-line support** with access to **human counselling**, along with mood tracking, journaling, mental-health resources, and peer-support features.

The main goal is to provide students with a safe and accessible way to seek initial support without depending entirely on face-to-face counselling.

---

## 1.3 Proposed Approach

MindCare follows a **hybrid AI + human support model**:

```text
Student
   ↓
AI Mental-Health Support
   ↓
Self-Help / Guided Exercises
   ↓
Further Support When Required
   ↓
Counsellor / Emergency Support
